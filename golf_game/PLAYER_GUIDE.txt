PINEWATER
A Golfistuen escape · Version 0.2.0

WELCOME
Extract the complete download, then double-click Pinewater (Linux),
Pinewater.exe (Windows), or Pinewater.app (macOS).
No Python, Godot editor, radar backend or internet connection is needed to play.

Choose "Enter the range" for practice, "Hole builder" to create a hole, or
"Play local golf" for a competitive round. "My holes" opens saved designs and the
included Pinewater Meadow sample; Play takes your selection into round setup.

LOCAL GOLF · ONE TO SIX PLAYERS
Choose names and colours, then choose a course. Click a face to preview that
player's animated square-headed golfer. Photo imports accept local PNG/JPEG/WebP
images, centre-crop them to a square, and save a small local copy; nothing uploads.
The colour button swaps colours so the players remain distinct. List order is
the agreed first-tee order, or choose Draw first-tee honours to randomise it.

Add your favourite playable holes to the course, reorder them with the arrows,
or remove them with ×. Favourites can repeat. Save course keeps the complete
ordered layouts, including cup sizes, even if the source holes are later edited.
Alternatively, generate 1–36 original holes using a chosen seed and cup diameter.
Choose a saved course next time to replay the same layouts.

The six-player score strip stays above the playing view. The highlighted player
is up. Each player has an independent ball, lie and score. After everyone takes
their first tee stroke, the farthest ball from the cup plays next. Water/OOB
re-tees wait until the other players have taken their initial tee strokes.
Lowest gross score on the previous hole has honours on the next tee; ties retain
the previous tee order. A scorecard appears when everyone finishes the hole.
Click Scorecard or a player score at any time to see the full round. Equal final
totals share the win. Live strip totals include current-hole strokes; to-par
figures and scorecard totals cover completed holes (* marks provisional strokes).

Maximum Score is the default: announce double par, par + 5, or 12 strokes per
hole before the round. Give up hole (also R) asks for confirmation and records
that maximum. Ordinary stroke play has no cap: giving up records DNF and makes
that player's round unclassified. A pickup never becomes an artificially low score.

RELIEF / DROP
When the ball is obstructed, choose Relief / drop:
  Stroke and distance: previous stroke position, +1 penalty.
  Lateral: within two virtual club-lengths (2.4 m), no nearer the cup, +1.
  Back on line: behind the ball on the cup-to-ball line, +1.
  Outside a bunker, back on line: +2. One-stroke bunker relief stays in that bunker.
  Fairway rescue: a painted fairway point, no nearer the cup, +2. This is an
    optional SIMULATOR HOUSE RULE, announced in setup, not free standard relief.
Click the map to select a point, use the wheel to zoom, and right-drag to pan.
White marks the old lie; green marks a valid selected drop. Trees are shown too.
Take relief applies the penalty and retains the selected player's turn. Invalid
drops cannot be confirmed. Water/OOB already use the shot plus one penalty and
return to the previous safe lie; selectable penalty-area drop options are not used.

No putting: green +0–3 additional strokes is announced in setup (default +2).
A directly holed shot adds none. These are simulator gross-score competitions:
there are no handicaps, match-play concessions, putting or full tournament rulings.
Round rules stay fixed even if general settings change during play.

COURSE GUIDE · DISTANCES AT A GLANCE
The left-side map shows the actual course from above before and during a shot:
fairway, green, water, bunkers, trees, OOB, tee, pin and the other players' balls.
Distance rings start at the current shot lie. The dashed gold line shows aim;
the moving marker and gold trail show the actual shot. Pin distance updates live.
Front/back distances estimate the cup green's edges on the 2 m course grid.
Hover or click a lay-up point to see distance out and remaining distance to pin.
Right-click clears the measurement; measuring does not change aim or take a shot.
Distances follow Settings > metres/yards. Use − / + to collapse or expand the map.
The driving range has the same guide, with its actual 50–300 m target positions.
All map distances are horizontal plan distances, not slope-adjusted club advice.

Rounds save automatically at shot start, after each result/drop/pickup, and on hole
transitions. Resume saved round restores the course, players, scores and turn.
An interrupted in-flight shot re-simulates from launch without counting it again.
Only one active round is retained; starting another replaces it. Completed
scorecards are archived locally. Replay never changes scores or turn order.

HOLE BUILDER
New designs start as an empty rough plot with the same alpine backdrop.
Paint Fairway, Green, Rough, Water, Sand or OOB. Use Raise/Lower/Smooth/Flatten
to sculpt hills and valleys. Place the tee, a cup on the green, and trees.

Left drag      Paint / sculpt
Right/middle   Pan
Wheel         Zoom
F             Frame the plot
Plan / 3D     Toggle angled preview
Ctrl+Z / Y    Undo / redo a complete stroke
Ctrl+S        Save to My holes

Give the design a name and par. Test this hole enters play; Save adds it to your
library. Import/Export share hole JSON files. Your work is stored in per-user
application data, outside the installation folder. A separate draft is retained
when leaving the builder.

Cup diameter is a map setting in the inspector (10.8–300 cm, default 30 cm).
Choose a larger opening to compensate for simulator detection inaccuracies.
The visible recessed hole and capture physics use the same size, and the ball
drops inside when hit. Cup size is saved, exported and recorded with results.

CUSTOM HOLES · NO PUTTING
Use Aim at cup or the Aim slider, then launch a shot. Each next shot starts from
the last resting lie. Green, fairway, rough and sand have different behaviour;
tree trunks can deflect the ball.

A ball that stops on the green completes the hole with two ADDITIONAL automatic
strokes by default. Example: two real shots onto the green plus two automatic
strokes gives a score of four. A directly holed shot adds no automatic strokes.
There is no putting phase. Change the count under Settings > Simulator rules.

Water and OOB count the shot plus a configurable penalty (default +1), then
return to the previous safe lie. White stakes mark the outer course boundary.
Rules are fixed for each attempt; Restart hole applies changed settings.
Results show real shots, penalties and automatic strokes separately.
Replay never changes the score. Completed gross scores and rule snapshots are
retained in local history, ready for future scoring features.

DEBUG / REAL SHOT INPUT
In "The next shot", choose Debug for simulated shots or Real for live radar.
This works on the range, custom holes and in local competitive golf.

Debug: choose Driver, 7 Iron or Wedge, adjust the sliders and click Launch shot
or press Space.

Real: start the Rust radar app in live mode on the radar computer. Enter its IP
address (127.0.0.1 if it runs on this computer) and port 8765 in the game, then
click Connect / reconnect. Wait for "READY — SHOOT NOW" before swinging.
Aim in the game and hit your real ball: a registered shot fires automatically.
In local golf, the highlighted player receives the shot. Wait for the next ready
indication before shooting again. Mode changes are disabled while a shot is busy.

The game reconnects automatically if the connection drops. Paused, busy or old
shots cannot fire later. The current radar estimator supplies speed, direction
and elevation; it does not measure spin yet. Replay never adds another stroke.
Your selected mode and radar address are saved on this computer.

RADAR DEBUG RANGE — 3D CAPTURE

Choose Radar debug range from the main menu to inspect actual radar measurements.
Connect to the same radar host/port used by Real mode. Raw points include background,
people, club returns and reflections; they are sparse measurements, not camera video.

Right-drag to orbit, middle-drag to pan, and use the wheel to zoom. Top/Side/Front
and Fit change the camera. Space plays/pauses; Left/Right step frames. Use the
timeline and slow-motion selector to inspect each attempt, including unconfirmed
hits. Click a point for coordinates, Doppler, SNR and its point-gate reason. Choose
a candidate trajectory to see its recorded rejection and fit diagnostics.

Green is the selected ball track, purple is inferred club evidence, orange is the
inspected candidate and gold is the fitted model. Raw unclassified points are not
automatically labelled objects. The metre grid is at radar Z=0, not a calibrated floor.

Mark live hit (M) or Dry swing immediately after an attempt to preserve a label and
the last three seconds even if detection never triggered. A connected mark is also
saved in the radar recording. Export selected clip saves a portable JSON movie.
Open clip replays it on another computer; Open folder loads a recording's event files.
The source selector/refresh button browses recordings on the radar computer over LAN.

Pausing playback keeps capture running. Follow live (L) returns to current frames;
All loaded frames browses the whole loaded buffer/recording. The debug mode does
not launch golf shots or add strokes. Escape returns to the main menu.

SIDE SPIN
Use the Side spin slider for left/right curvature. Negative RPM bends left,
positive RPM bends right, and zero is neutral. This is independent of the initial
launch direction. There is no backspin/topspin input. The flight guide and replay
use the same side-spin model as the live shot.

Space       Launch a simulated shot (Debug mode)
1 / 2 / 3   Driver / iron / wedge
R           Range: fresh ball; solo test: restart; competition: confirm pickup
P           Replay the last shot
Escape      Pause / back
F11         Fullscreen

The flight guide previews the actual flight model. Carry is the horizontal
distance to first landing. Total includes the bounce and rollout. Replay does
not add another shot to the session history.

The camera starts directly behind the tee/aiming line and always follows the ball,
keeping it framed and easing into a closer
view as it lands and rolls. Objects blocking the ball smoothly become transparent
instead of pushing or rotating the camera. The rendered ball rests on the visible turf surface.
The soft flight trail follows the ball and quickly fades after landing.

SETTINGS
Open Settings in the menu or on the range. Fullscreen, graphics quality, distance
units, camera behavior, ball trail, and volume preferences are saved on your
computer. Reduced camera motion keeps follow-ball movements restrained.

GRAPHICS
Recommended: a current Vulkan-capable GPU, 8 GB RAM, and a 1080p display.
Low and Medium quality lower render resolution, vegetation and shadow cost.
The view is kept clear of lens flashes, noisy cutouts and bloom overlays.
On older graphics hardware, the Linux OpenGL launcher or the command-line option
--rendering-method gl_compatibility selects the compatibility renderer.

APPLICATION DATA
Preferences are saved in Godot's standard per-user application data directory,
under Pinewater. They do not require write access to the installation folder.
Practice-shot history lasts for the current application session.
Hole designs are under holes/. Players, square portraits, favourite courses,
the active-round checkpoint and completed-round archives are under clubhouse/.
